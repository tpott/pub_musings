Welcome to my blog! This is my first post on my new personal website.

## What to Expect

I plan to write about:

- **Software engineering** - thoughts on building software, architecture, and best practices
- **Technology** - interesting tools, languages, and frameworks I'm exploring
- **Projects** - updates on personal projects and experiments

I don't have lofty expectations like daily or weekly posts here. But I am hoping for at least _monthly_, i.e. twelve posts for 2026.

## Why a Blog?

I want to improve at writing and communicating my ideas. Reading other people's blogs has been a great way for me to learn and I'm sure those authors learned a lot by the process of writing. Additionally, there has been a lot of changes to the tech industry over the last 1-3 years. I hope that starting a blog will help me stand out, or at least give me more opportunities to learn.

## Technical Details

This site is built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), a modern static site generator. I liked how simple it was to add new markdown files for blog posts. The source code is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/tpott/pub_musings/tree/trunk/personal).

Thanks for reading, and stay tuned for more posts!
